Welcome to ClubMadeira.io

Club Madeira is a weekends-mostly affiliate club. Partners introduce merchants and community clubs, then promote “buy from my club” so members shop through the Smart Catalogue and clubs raise funds from commission. It is not a dump of catalogues without promotion.

This partner kit is a set of static HTML pages that load Club Madeira widgets from our CDN. Host the files as they are, replace the example affiliateCode in index.json with your own, and the header menu, login, signup and dashboard will talk to the live API. Public visitors can browse clubs, merchants, partners and vouchers. Signed-in partners manage profile, clubs and account settings; merchants manage parts and API keys.

Self-serve signup on this site attributes new merchants and communities to the site owner’s affiliateCode. That is separate from a partner or admin invite (Stripe Express onboarding and the Users.referrer tree) and from account handoff on the Account page. Speak to a Club Madeira partner if you need an onboarding invite.
